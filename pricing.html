Pricing questions

Questions about payment

Is the trial class really free?

Yes. The first class is a free 30-minute trial with no payment details required. It lets your child meet the tutor and lets us place them at the right level before you decide anything.

How do I pay?

Plans are billed monthly. At launch we will accept card payments, PayPal and SEPA Direct Debit — the standard way to pay for subscriptions in Germany. Prices are shown in USD and the EUR equivalent is charged at checkout.

Can I cancel or pause my plan?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no lock-in. You can cancel or pause for holidays at any time, and the plan simply does not renew the following month.
