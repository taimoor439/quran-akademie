Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do the online classes work?

Every class is live and 1-to-1 with your tutor on Zoom or Google Meet. Each lesson is 25–45 minutes, depending on your plan: your tutor shares the lesson on screen, listens to your recitation, corrects pronunciation gently, and sets a short practice task for the week.

What time are the classes in Germany?

Classes run in Germany time (CET/CEST). The most popular slots are after school and in the evening, between 3pm and 8pm, plus weekend mornings. You choose the days and times that fit your family when you book.

What do we need for the first class?

A laptop, tablet or phone with a camera, a stable internet connection, and a quiet place to sit. Headphones help but are not required. Your tutor will send the meeting link before the class — no software to install beyond Zoom or Google Meet.
