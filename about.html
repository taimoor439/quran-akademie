About Quran Akademie

Quality Quran education, taught live and one-to-one

We help Muslim families in Germany learn the Quran with confidence — live lessons, caring certified tutors, and a structured path from the first letter to confident recitation.

Our mission

Quran learning that fits real family life in Germany

Many Muslim families in Germany want their children to learn the Quran properly — with correct pronunciation, real understanding, and a love for the words of Allah. But finding a qualified teacher who fits around school, work and family life is not always easy.

Quran Akademie exists to solve that. Our lessons are taught live, one-to-one, online — so your child learns from a certified tutor in the comfort of your home, at a time that suits your family, in Germany time. Every lesson is personal: the tutor knows your child's level, pace and goals, and every class builds on the last.

Whether your child is taking their first steps with the Arabic alphabet or working towards Hifz, our aim is the same: steady progress, correct Tajweed, and a warm connection with the Quran that lasts a lifetime.

Our teaching method

Gentle, structured and level-based

Every student is placed at the right level and follows a clear learning path. Lessons are calm and encouraging — especially for children — with short, focused steps that build real confidence.

15 minutes

Warm-up & revision

We begin by revising the previous lesson, so new learning always rests on a solid foundation.

215 minutes

New lesson

The tutor introduces the new material — letters, words or verses — step by step, with careful correction of pronunciation and Tajweed.

37 minutes

Practice & reading

The student reads aloud, practises the new material and asks questions. The tutor gives gentle, clear feedback throughout.

43 minutes

Wrap-up & homework

The lesson ends with a short summary, a small practice task for the week, and a word of encouragement.

Lesson length depends on your plan — 25, 30 or 45 minutes — and can be adjusted to suit the student.

What makes us different

Built around your family, not around a timetable

  • ✓

    Certified tutors

  • ✓

    Safeguarding first

    Every tutor is identity-checked and safeguarding-trained. Lessons take place only on approved platforms, and a parent or guardian stays nearby during children's lessons.

Our values

What guides every lesson

Ikhlaas — sincerity

We teach the Quran for the sake of Allah, with honesty in everything we do.

Sabur — patience

Every child learns at their own pace. We never rush, compare or pressure.

Ihsaan — excellence

Correct Tajweed, careful preparation and lessons we would be proud for our own children to attend.

Amanah — trust

Parents trust us with their children. We honour that trust with safeguarding, transparency and care.
