Tafseer & Understanding

Understand what you recite — meanings, context and lessons from the Quran, in plain language.

Course overview

Reciting the Quran is beautiful — understanding it changes your life. This course opens up the meaning of the Quran in clear, plain language, so every verse you recite connects with your heart and your daily life.

Lessons are taught in English or German (your choice) and draw on classical, well-established Tafseer. We explore the context of revelation, the key Arabic words, and the practical lessons each passage holds for life in Europe today.

What you will learn

  • The meaning of Juz' Amma and other short surahs, verse by verse
  • Asbab al-Nuzul — the context in which verses were revealed
  • Key Arabic words and how they shape the meaning
  • The main themes of the Quran — mercy, justice, patience, gratitude
  • Practical lessons for everyday life in Germany
  • How to reflect (tadabbur) on the verses you recite in prayer

Who this course is for

This course is for teens and adults who already recite — even a little — and want to understand what they are saying. No Arabic is required; lessons are in English or German.

  • Adults who recite in prayer and want to understand the words
  • Teens curious about the meaning behind the surahs
  • New Muslims building their understanding step by step
  • Hifz students who want meaning alongside memorisation

Class format

  • 1-to-1 live lessons with a dedicated tutor
  • 25–45 minutes per class, depending on your plan
  • Zoom or Google Meet — link sent before class
  • Germany time (CET/CEST)

FAQ

Questions about this course

Do I need to know Arabic to join this course?+

No. Lessons are taught in English or German, and key Arabic words are explained as we go. Many students find that Tafseer actually improves their Arabic vocabulary over time.

Which Tafseer books do you use?+

We draw on classical, widely respected sources — such as Tafseer Ibn Kathir and Tafseer al-Jalalayn — presented in accessible, everyday language rather than academic detail.

Can I choose which surahs we study?+

Yes — after the opening units, you can request specific surahs or themes with your tutor. Many students start with the surahs they recite most often in prayer.
